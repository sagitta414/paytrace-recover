# Five-seller research kit

Status: interview plan only. No sellers have been contacted or interviewed. No willingness-to-pay or demand evidence exists yet.

Recruit five independent sellers who accept PayPal for digital courses or memberships and maintain access separately. Ask for a conversation about a past incident before showing the demo. Do not request customer payment credentials or unredacted personal records.

## Questions

1. Tell me about the last time a customer paid but could not access what they bought. What happened, and when?
2. Walk me through the actual investigation. Which records and tools did you open? What was uncertain? How much time did it take?
3. How often has this happened in the last 90 days? What evidence supports that estimate? What did the customer do next?
4. What fixes or tools have you already tried? Why do you still handle any part manually?
5. After seeing the prototype: what would you need to trust one proposed access grant? Would you allow a supervised sandbox trial using test purchases in your setup?

Avoid asking “Would you use this?” as the main validation. Ask about recent behavior, existing spend, specific integration constraints, and an actual next step. Do not turn an enthusiastic comment into a purchase commitment.

## Decision rule

Continue toward a real course-platform connector if at least three of five sellers can describe recent relevant incidents and at least two agree to a supervised test with a concrete setup. These are initial research thresholds, not statistically representative market proof. If incidents are rare, already handled well, or integration access is impractical, revisit the customer segment before expanding the product.
